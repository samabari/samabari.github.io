# Change 1: Strengthen the professional introduction

## Goal

Improve the Home page introduction so recruiters quickly understand the portfolio owner's product marketing and growth marketing background and the value that combination brings.

## What we plan to change

- Refine the headline and lead paragraph to state the product marketing and growth marketing focus more clearly.
- Sharpen the through-line connecting customer insight, positioning, experimentation, launches, and measurable product growth.
- Keep the change limited to the introductory copy. Preserve the current page structure, navigation, proof points, and verified facts; do not add unsupported claims or metrics.

## Why this improves the portfolio

- Recruiters can identify relevant product marketing and growth marketing experience at a glance.
- A clearer through-line helps connect the portfolio's range of roles to a coherent professional profile.
- A stronger introduction gives recruiting readers a clearer reason to continue to the experience and outcomes shown elsewhere on the site.
